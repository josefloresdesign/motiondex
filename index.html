The psychology of motion

Why good animation works

Good motion isn't decoration. It tells people what just happened, where to look next and that something is working behind the scenes. These studies show where it helps, and where it doesn't.

What the research says

UX laws that motion supports

The other side

Animation won't save a bad experience

It nudges and explains. It can't fix a confusing flow.

  • Many studies found animated graphics teach no better than good static ones. Tversky, Morrison & Bétrancourt, 2002 ↗
  • Movement that has nothing to do with the task pulls attention away and makes things harder to learn. Educational Psychology Review ↗
  • Motion can make some people dizzy or sick. Always respect the “reduce motion” setting. web.dev ↗
